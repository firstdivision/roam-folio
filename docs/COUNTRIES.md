# Approved answers and guessable countries

## Status

**Approved on 2026-10-24 and expanded on 2026-10-25.** The original 125-country list, Chad addition and territory policy are retained. The user approved 20 more answers and made the other 51 countries from the reviewed exclusions table guess-only. Current registry: `data/countries/pool.v3.json`; v1 and v2 remain for immutable published answer cycles.

The registry contains **197 guessable countries**, of which **146 can be answers** and **51 are guess-only**. Every registry entry has an explicit boolean `answerEligible`. All 197 can be guessed immediately and receive distance/color feedback. Guess-only countries never appear as daily targets and do not unlock postcards. Separate territories and other map entries are excluded, as explicitly confirmed by the user.

Each answer appears once per full cycle for its pool version, without weighting. Published 125-country cycles run through 2032-11-04; the 126-country cycle runs from 2032-11-05 through 2033-03-10. The 146-country answer pool begins on **2033-03-11**, with its first cycle ending on 2033-08-03. All 2,626 pre-expansion assignments, era versions and saved-progress identities are preserved. Countries outside the guess registry remain neutral on the map.

## Selection approach

- Favor recognizable countries across regions rather than automatically enabling every geographic feature.
- Include familiar Caribbean and Oceania countries; do not categorically exclude island countries.
- The initial pool used the documented 100–130 range; the explicitly approved expansion now has 146 answers.
- Keep guess eligibility separate from answer eligibility; the wider guess list supplies clues without expanding the answer pool automatically.
- Treat the grouping below as an editorial aid, not a geopolitical classification or a claim about recognition.
- Country omissions reflect MVP scope, not a judgment about sovereignty, culture, or importance.

The IDs below are stable application identifiers using familiar three-letter country codes. Do not assume they match every Natural Earth field: the geographic pipeline uses the verified source-feature/component crosswalk in `data/geography/crosswalk.v3.json`. An application's country name, source label, and sovereign identifier need not be identical.

## Typed names and aliases

Canonical names above remain valid typed guesses. The registry will include reviewed common aliases such as USA / US / United States of America, UK / Britain / Great Britain, Czech Republic, Türkiye, UAE, DR Congo / DRC, and Republic of Korea. Chad accepts Republic of Chad, Tchad, and République du Tchad. Additional reviewed aliases include Holy See / Vatican (VAT), State of Palestine / PSE (PSX), SSD (SDS), Kosova / XKX (KOS), Ivory Coast (CIV), Swaziland (SWZ), East Timor (TLS), and Federated States of Micronesia (FSM). Aliases identify the game country and do not change its geometry.

Do not guess automatically from ambiguous names such as "Congo" or "Korea"; ask the player to select a specific country name from the typed suggestions. Normalize case and surrounding whitespace. Unknown or ineligible input receives a clear explanation.

## Geographic mapping

The approved treatment of islands, overseas regions, dependencies, and disputed boundaries is in [GEOGRAPHY.md](GEOGRAPHY.md). In particular, an eligible country is not automatically the union of every territory with the same sovereign identifier.

The source-component crosswalk has been verified against checksummed Natural Earth 5.1.1 archives. See `data/geography/crosswalk.v3.json` and the recorded Norway, Finland, and Egypt handling in GEOGRAPHY.md.

## Changes and approval

The current list is recorded as the versioned `pool.v3.json` registry, an additive extension of v2 with explicit answer eligibility. Both original registries remain unchanged. Amendments require approval. A future pool change begins at a future cycle boundary beyond already published puzzle assignments; it never rewrites an existing date.
