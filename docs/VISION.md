# Product Vision — Roamfolio

## One-sentence pitch
A daily geography puzzle where discovering the country is just the beginning.

## Experience
Roamfolio feels like a small journey: guess a destination, follow geographic clues, arrive, and receive a postcard full of interesting things to learn and eat. The design should feel like travel and exploration, not a school exam.

## Differentiation
- A shared daily guessing ritual.
- Immediate numeric distance and map proximity clues.
- A curated set of recognizable countries to keep difficulty enjoyable.
- A satisfying reveal featuring cultural facts, landmarks, signature foods, and optional recipes.
- A growing digital passport of places discovered.

## Audience and tone
Casual players who enjoy travel, maps, food, and learning about the world. Inviting, curious, polished, and never condescending.

## Guiding principles
- Easy to start, satisfying to finish.
- A fair daily challenge for everyone.
- Accuracy and cultural respect over generic trivia.
- Beautiful on mobile and desktop.
- Expandable without complicating the first release.

## Brand
Working name: **Roamfolio**. Working tagline: **Discover a country. Collect its story.** Potential domain: `roamfolio.world` (not confirmed purchased).
