Acknowledgements and data licenses

Map and geographic boundaries

Made with Natural Earth. The Admin 0 countries and map-unit datasets, version 5.1.1, are public domain. Roamfolio applies its documented territory mapping, repairs a self-touching source ring in Egypt without moving vertices, simplifies and rounds the display derivative, and calculates WGS84 boundary distances offline. Country boundaries follow the selected release and are not an assertion of current legal sovereignty. All displayed distances are approximate.
