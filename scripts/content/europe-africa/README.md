# Europe and Africa discovery research

Reviewed 2026-10-24. The artifact is `data/discovery/europe-africa.json`: 61 cards covering the 36 Europe and 25 Africa IDs in the approved country pool. Each card contains three facts, a cultural note, and one food to discover, with a source URL and title on every claim. Descriptions are original concise factual summaries, not copied article passages. No images or recipes are included.

## Basic facts

Capital, language, region and landlocked/coastal facts were inspected in the country records of **mledoze/countries**, pinned at commit `6031f5df137c41c76d180c55a0782185ae3ed05b`. The JSON and license were fetched over HTTPS and checksummed; their SHA-256 values and immutable URLs are recorded in `source-manifest.json`. The complete source license is retained in `LICENSE.mledoze-countries.txt` (ODbL 1.0).

The artifact is a small selection and transformation of those fields, combined with separately researched cultural/food summaries. Retain the mledoze/countries contributor attribution, immutable source link, ODbL notice and this modification notice when redistributing the country-reference extraction. The source database and its country classifications are editorial reference material; they do not define Roamfolio's eligible-country geometry or territory policy. No populations, current leaders, price information or official-language counts are copied.

Language statements describe some languages used in the country; they do not claim an exhaustive list or current legal status. This avoids turning stale source language classifications (for example in Mali or South Africa) into current legal claims. Austria's source language is normalized to German; Switzerland's Swiss German is summarized as German. Namibia uses the precise Ndonga name recorded in the source. Norwegian written forms are retained explicitly. Regional labels are omitted for transcontinental Russia and countries with broader overseas-component policies. For Switzerland, a consulted Swiss federal-government source confirms Bern's precise status as federal city rather than asserting a de jure capital.

## Culture and food

Every country food entry was checked against the cited Wikivoyage country's Eat section; culture notes were checked against the named section or a separate cited source. For Albania, Belarus, DR Congo, Uganda and Zambia, UNESCO's intangible-heritage descriptions or safeguarding-project pages were consulted. Egypt, Sudan and Tanzania use consulted UNESCO World Heritage Centre descriptions. Ethiopia uses the Ethiopian Coffee and Tea Authority's coffee booklet. Ukraine uses UNESCO's own Petrykivka video description because the canonical UNESCO page returned an anti-bot screen. Switzerland's federal-city fact uses the Swiss Federal Department of Foreign Affairs.

Wikivoyage material is by Wikivoyage contributors under CC BY-SA 4.0; each source article links its contributor/edit history. The consulted license policy is https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:Copyleft and the license is https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The summaries are rewritten, condensed, and selectively scoped. Retain this attribution and modification notice, source links, and the license link for those portions. UNESCO World Heritage short descriptions state CC BY-SA IGO 3.0 on their pages; source/license links are retained in the manifest. Other sources supply factual reference only; no permission to reproduce their prose or media is asserted.

Food entries deliberately present one dish, ingredient, or preparation to discover. They do not claim that a food is exclusive to one country or the country's single official/national dish. Variations and shared regional traditions are expected. No medical advice, entry requirements, live prices, geopolitical opinions, or unsupported travel claims from the reference pages are reused.

## Review limitations

This is deliberately a minimal first-playable content set. Its three basic facts emphasize capitals, languages and geography, while the cultural and food notes supply more specific discovery. Cultural notes include foodways as well as music, craft, architecture and social traditions. A later editorial pass can replace basic facts with more varied historical or scientific stories while preserving provenance.

All 61 IDs and all 305 claim records were checked for schema/source completeness. This research pass checked claim support in structured source records or retrieved page excerpts; it is not independent specialist review and does not assert that every statement in a cited source is correct. Source pages can change after retrieval; the primary country dataset is pinned, while article and institution links are dated references rather than archived snapshots.
